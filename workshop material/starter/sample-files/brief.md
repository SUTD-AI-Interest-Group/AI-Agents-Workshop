# Workshop brief

**Help SUTD students find the right project teammates.**

Every term, students form teams for design projects (e.g. 3.007, capstone, hackathons).
Teams are often formed in a rush, by friendship or seating, and skills or schedules don't match.

Constraints for the pilot:
- Must be usable within 4 weeks by a small student team.
- Pilot with ONE student club or ONE cohort class first.
- No access to official student records.
